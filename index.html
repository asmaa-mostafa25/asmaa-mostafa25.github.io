Featured case study

BloodFlow — moving blood before it expires.

Hospitals run short of blood while units expire in other banks. BloodFlow connects hospitals, blood banks, donors and recipients across Egypt, tracks every batch, and reserves the units that expire first.

Graduation project · Team Leader & Backend

What I built

  • Leading a 9-member team across backend, frontend, UI/UX and AI, and owning the ASP.NET Core Web API.
  • Four-layer Clean Architecture: Domain, Application, Infrastructure, Api.
  • Identity + JWT with 5 roles, account lockout, staff approval workflow, audit log and an active-account check on every request.
  • Batch inventory with a stock ledger, scheduled expiry job and FEFO reservations for normal and emergency requests.
  • Integration with our AI service for demand forecasting, shortage and expiry alerts.
  • RFC 9457 ProblemDetails errors; GitHub Actions runs build, tests and a vulnerable-package check on every PR.
